## Signature interaction and motion grammar

Signature: the live notch card. Requests drop from the slot with a short spring, a countdown ring drains, pressing a scope key travels 2px with a click-latch and the card retracts into the slot; the next request follows. Keyboard: Enter allows, Esc denies, Tab cycles scope. Motion grammar: mechanical (short travel, firm easing, no float/glow), respects prefers-reduced-motion (cards appear without travel; no auto-cycle).
