# Design System: loggdin

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Braun Control Panel"**

The site is a single piece of anodised hardware. The ground is warm grey aluminium; content sits either on a raised faceplate or in machined recesses cut into the plate; every boundary is an engraved hairline that catches light on its lower lip. Controls are physical: detented keys that travel 2px when pressed and latch, small status lamps, a perforated speaker grille drawn from an 8px dot field. One orange key per view carries the primary action. Nothing floats, nothing glows.

Density is calm and instrument-like: generous section padding, short measured copy, and an 8px panel grid that governs every gap, inset and grille pitch. The type is one variable grotesque (Archivo) set slightly condensed and heavy for legends, like screen-printed panel lettering, with the system monospace kept for code and terminal text only. A black-anodised variant (Braun made them too) follows `prefers-color-scheme: dark` by swapping the plate and ink tokens; the geometry, signal orange and lamps are unchanged.

There is one deliberate exception to the aluminium world: the notch card. It mirrors the real macOS product's dark-glass approval card faithfully, including its system-blue Allow key, its graphite Deny key and its pill-segmented scope radiogroup, and it lives inside a black bezel with a notch slot. The card is the product shown truthfully, not a component of the panel language; its colours do not leak out onto the faceplate.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm grey anodised plate, raised faceplate, recessed machined panels.
- Engraved hairline rules (dark line plus a light lower lip) as the only dividers.
- 8px panel grid for spacing, grille pitch and lamp size.
- Detented keys with press travel and a latched state; one orange signal key per view.
- Archivo variable, condensed and heavy for legends; system monospace for code only.
- Mechanical motion: short travel, firm easing, no float, no glow; reduced motion removes travel.
- Black-anodised dark variant by token swap only.
- The notch card is a faithful mirror of the macOS product, the one dark-glass, system-blue exception.

## Colors

A warm, low-chroma aluminium palette with charcoal legends, one hot signal orange, and lamp colours reserved for status.

### Primary
- **Signal Orange** (signal): the one orange key. Primary action only (Join the waitlist), the mascot's face in the wordmark and card, the focus ring, the text caret, selection tint (35% mix) and hover underlines. Its legend is **Burnt Charcoal** (on-signal), never white.
- **Backlit Signal** (signal-press): the signal key's hover. The cap lightens, like a backlit key, rather than darkening.

### Secondary
- **Lamp Green** (lamp-green): lit status lamps, success form notes and the low-risk badge. A lamp, not an accent; never fills a key or panel.
- **Risk Amber** (risk-med) and **Risk Red** (risk-high): the risk badges in the notch card; red also marks invalid input and error notes.

### Tertiary (notch card only)
- **Allow Blue** (allow-blue): the Allow key, mirroring the macOS app. Exists only inside the notch card.
- **Deny Graphite** (deny-graphite), **Card Glass** (card), **Screen Black** (screen), **Bezel Black** (bezel), **Card Ink** (card-ink, card-ink-2): the device and dark-glass card. These are fixed in both colour schemes because they depict a real screen.

### Bezel palette (scoped)
These colours are part of the depicted product screen. They are valid only inside the device bezel and do not change with the colour scheme.
- **Card controls:** Allow hover (bezel-allow-hover), Deny hover (bezel-deny-hover), and the command and scope well (bezel-well).
- **Countdown ring:** Ring Blue (bezel-ring) while time remains, switching to Ring Amber (bezel-ring-warn) in the last 3 seconds.
- **Risk chip:** the medium-risk amber chip takes Amber Ink (bezel-risk-med-ink). Low and high chips use white ink.
- **Command text:** Command Blue (bezel-cmd), inside the command well.
- **Editor and terminal:** base text (bezel-code), file tree (bezel-tree), keyword (bezel-syntax-keyword), string (bezel-syntax-string), function (bezel-syntax-function), and log allow/deny lines (bezel-log-ok, bezel-log-no).

### Neutral
- **Anodised Plate** (plate): page ground.
- **Raised Plate** (plate-hi): the faceplate, key faces and step buttons.
- **Machined Recess** (plate-lo): recessed sections, the field well, the footer, inline code ground. Exposed to components as `--panel`.
- **Deep Recess** (plate-deep): grille perforations, jack sockets, and `--panel` inside an already recessed section.
- **Legend Charcoal** (ink): headings, body, key legends; inverse plate for the Deny keycap and the hub.
- **Secondary Legend** (ink-2): ledes, paragraphs, nav links.
- **Tertiary Legend** (ink-3): captions, fine print, placeholders, scrollbar thumb.
- **Engraving** (rule): every hairline divider, paired with a translucent white lower lip (`rgba(255,255,255,0.7)` light, `0.06` dark).
- **Unlit Lamp** (lamp-off): off-state lamps.

The black-anodised variant replaces plate, plate-hi, plate-lo, plate-deep, ink, ink-2, ink-3, rule and lamp-off with their `dark-*` counterparts, deepens the key shadows and darkens the bezel to `#0a0a0a`. Signal, lamps, risk colours and the card palette do not change.

### Named Rules
**The One Orange Key Rule.** Signal orange fills exactly one key per view, and that key is the primary action. Secondary actions are plate-coloured keys.

**The Lamps Are Status Rule.** Green, amber and red appear only as lamps, risk badges and form state. They are never decoration.

**The Glass Stays In The Bezel Rule.** Allow blue, card glass and the dark editor palette exist only inside the device bezel. The aluminium world never borrows them.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo variable (self-hosted, OFL; weight 100-900, width 62-125%) with ui-sans-serif, system-ui
**Body Font:** Archivo variable
**Label/Mono Font:** Archivo for labels; ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas for code, commands and the terminal only

**Character:** One grotesque used like panel lettering: condensed (`font-stretch` 88-94%) and heavy for legends, normal width and a light 420 weight for reading, with stylistic set ss01 on throughout.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (660, clamp 2.7-4.75rem, 0.95, stretch 88%, -0.04em): the hero headline only; capped at 12ch when single-column.
- **Headline** (640, clamp 2rem-3.4rem, 1.05, stretch 90%, -0.035em): section titles, max 18ch, balanced wrap. Legal pages use clamp 2.4-3.6rem at the same stretch.
- **Title** (640, 1.3-1.6rem, 1.05, -0.02em): step, tier and download titles; FAQ summaries at 600, 1.08rem.
- **Body** (420, 1.0625rem, 1.55): paragraphs at 40-66ch measures; section ledes 1.12rem at 60ch; hero lede is the fluid lede style.
- **Label** (640, 0.82rem, 0.04em, uppercase, stretch 92%): spec-plate terms only. Legend lines under the bezel are 0.86rem at stretch 94%, sentence case.
- **Key** (600, 0.98rem, 1.2): every key legend. Keycaps in the key bank use 650, 1.05rem.
- **Code** (system mono, 0.88em): inline code sits in a recessed chip (plate-lo, 1px rule border, 4px radius).

### Observed size ramp by role
Every step the build uses, grouped by role. Use these steps and add no new ones.
- **Display:** hero clamp(2.7rem, 1rem + 4.1vw, 4.75rem); legal h1 clamp(2.4rem, 1.6rem + 2.4vw, 3.6rem); section h2 clamp(2rem, 1.3rem + 2.4vw, 3.4rem).
- **Title:** 1.6rem (tier), 1.4rem (legal h2), 1.35rem (download), 1.3rem (step titles, wordmark), 1.15rem (hub name), 1.08rem (FAQ summary), 1.05rem (keycaps).
- **Body:** 1.0625rem base; 1.12rem section lede; fluid hero lede clamp(1.1rem, 0.95rem + 0.45vw, 1.3rem); 1rem field input; 0.98rem (key legend, notes, spec values); 0.97rem (tier and download copy); 0.95rem (nav, footer links, asides, step numbers).
- **Meta:** 0.93rem (keyspec copy), 0.92rem (jack labels), 0.9rem (form note, legal date), 0.88rem (tier state).
- **Spec/legend:** 0.86rem (bezel legend, demo caption), 0.84rem (fine print), 0.82rem (spec terms; jacks on phone), 0.78rem (hub sub-line).
- **Card (product mirror):** base clamp(12px, 0.95vw, 14.5px), re-scaled to clamp(12px, 2.1vw, 14.5px) at 1020px and below, and 13px on phones. Steps inside it are em-relative: 1.22em question, 1em command, 0.95em scope, 0.92em meta and resolved line, 0.88em phone scope, 0.78em kbd, and the risk chip at max(11.5px, 0.82em).
- **Terminal (product mirror, mono, line-height 1.7):** clamp(10px, 0.78vw, 12.5px) for the editor and terminal, re-scaled to clamp(10px, 1.7vw, 12.5px) for the editor at 1020px and below, and 11px for the terminal on phones.

**The Depicted Screen Rule.** The card and terminal sizes describe a miniature of the real product. They are valid only inside the bezel. The 10px floor applies only to decorative, aria-hidden editor text and is never a size for real content.

### Named Rules
**The Width Axis Rule.** Hierarchy is carried by width and weight together: the larger the legend, the narrower and heavier it is set. Body text stays at 100% width.

**The Mono Is For Machines Rule.** Monospace appears only where the content is literally code, a command, a path in the editor, a terminal line or a keyboard key. Never for labels or decoration.

## Layout

Everything sits on an 8px panel grid (`--u`); gaps and insets are multiples of it (16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64). Content is held in a 1200px wrap with a fluid gutter (16-48px). Sections are separated by an engraved top rule and padded clamp(64px, 10vw, 128px); sections alternate between the plate and a recessed plate-lo band.

The first viewport is one raised faceplate containing a two-column grid (11fr copy, 13fr device) with a bounded 8px perforated grille under the form. Content sections use asymmetric splits (5fr/6fr, 4fr/7fr for FAQ) or equal-column plates divided by engraved rules (tiers, downloads, a three-step signal path, a five-key bank).

Responsive rules:
- **At 1020px and below** every split and the hero collapse to a single `minmax(0, 1fr)` column (never bare `1fr`, so long code and commands cannot blow out the track); the key bank drops to three columns; card and editor type re-scale against viewport width.
- **At 720px and below** the phone rules apply: the first two nav links hide; the signal path becomes a vertical list with a vertical engraved connector; the key bank, tiers and downloads stack to one column with horizontal engravings; spec-plate rows stack; the faceplate tightens to 14px padding and 16px radius; the waitlist row splits into a full-width recessed input and a full-width 48px key; the editor tree hides; card kbd hints hide; the risk badge drops under the title; and the "All folders" scope swaps to the short label "Folders" (the accessible name stays "All folders").

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical and shallow: the system is a plate with things raised from it and recesses cut into it, lit from above. Raised elements get a 1px dark lower edge, a short tight drop and a light inset top lip; recesses get a soft inset shadow, a 1px rule ring and a light lip below. Large ambient shadows exist only on the device bezel and the notch card, because they depict a real screen object.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Key rest** (`--shadow-key`: `0 1px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.16), 0 4px 8px -4px rgba(0,0,0,.35), inset 0 1px 0 var(--rule-hi)`): keys, keycaps and numbered step buttons.
- **Key down** (`--shadow-key-down`: `0 1px 2px -1px rgba(0,0,0,.3), inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.12)`): pressed or latching keys.
- **Recess** (`--recess`: `inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.13), inset 0 0 0 1px var(--rule), 0 1px 0 var(--rule-hi)`): machined panels (patch bay, spec plate, tiers, downloads).
- **Faceplate** (`inset 0 0 0 1px var(--rule), inset 0 2px 0 var(--rule-hi), 0 1px 0 var(--rule-hi)`): the raised first-viewport plate.
- **Engraved rule** (`border: 1px solid var(--rule)` plus `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--rule-hi)`): every divider.
- **Bezel** (`0 30px 60px -30px rgba(0,0,0,.55)` plus inner lip): the device only.
- **Card** (`0 24px 48px -18px rgba(0,0,0,.75), 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.4)`): the notch card only.

### Named Rules
**The Cut Not Float Rule.** Panels are cut into the plate (`--recess`) or raised a millimetre from it (`--shadow-key`). Nothing on the aluminium hovers on a soft ambient shadow.

**The Engraving Rule.** Dividers are engraved: a dark hairline with a light lip. Never a lone grey line, never a filled bar.

## Shapes

Gently radiused machined forms. Keys are 6px, the field well 10px, recessed panels 12px, the key bank 14px, the faceplate and notch card 20px. Lamps, jack sockets and step buttons are true circles; the risk badge is a pill. The bezel is 22px on top and 14px at the foot, with a notch slot of 0 0 12px 12px. The grille is a bounded field of 1.9px dots on an 8px pitch, never a full-bleed texture. Inside the notch card, keys and scope segments follow the macOS card (12px keys, 9px segments, 12px wells).

Radius scale by component:
- 1px: FAQ plus and minus bars.
- 4px: inline code chip, focus ring corner, editor tree selection.
- 5px: kbd hints on card keys.
- 6px: keys, input inside the field well, the skip link and terminal tabs (6px 6px 0 0).
- 8px: phone waitlist input.
- 9px: scope segment.
- 10px: field well, hub, screen top corners (10px 10px 0 0).
- 12px: card inner wells and keys, recessed panels, the notch slot foot (0 0 12px 12px), bezel foot on phones.
- 14px: key bank, bezel foot.
- 16px: faceplate on phones.
- 18px: bezel top on phones.
- 20px: faceplate and notch card.
- 22px: bezel top.
- 999px: risk chip.
- 50%: lamps, jacks, step buttons.

## Components

### Keys
Detented switches: firm, short, audible-looking travel.
- **Shape:** gently radiused (6px), minimum 44px tall, 10px 18px padding, nowrap legend.
- **Default:** raised plate face, charcoal legend, key-rest shadow.
- **Signal:** orange face with charcoal legend; the primary action only. Hover lightens to Backlit Signal (signal-press), like a backlit cap.
- **Hover:** plate keys darken by mixing 8% ink into the face.
- **Press and latch:** `:active` and the scripted `.is-pressed` state translate 2px down and switch to the key-down shadow over 90ms on the mechanical curve; scripted presses hold for 140ms before the action fires.
- **Disabled:** 55% opacity, not-allowed cursor.
- **Phone:** full width, 48px tall, in the waitlist form.

### Waitlist field
- **Style:** a recessed well (plate-lo, 10px radius, inset shadow) holding a borderless input and the signal key, 6px apart.
- **Focus:** 2px signal outline, zero offset. **Invalid:** 2px risk-red outline; the status line beneath turns red (error) or green (ok).

### Lamps
- 8px circles. Off: unlit lamp grey with an inset shade. On: lamp green with a 2px 22% green halo ring and a lower inset shade. Used in the bezel legend and tier states.

### Recessed panels
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** `--panel` (plate-lo; plate-deep when the section itself is recessed).
- **Shadow Strategy:** `--recess`.
- **Internal Padding:** 24-32px on the grid; internal columns are divided by engraved vertical rules that become horizontal on phones.

### Faceplate
- Raised plate-hi, 20px radius, fluid 24-56px padding, faceplate shadow; holds the whole first viewport.

### Navigation
- An engraved top strip, 64px minimum, wordmark (700, stretch 92%) left, links right at 0.95rem in secondary legend; hover to ink; GitHub link in ink at 560. Phone hides the first two section links.

### FAQ
- Engraved-rule disclosure rows; a plus drawn from two 14px bars rotates to minus over 220ms; bars turn orange on hover.

### Notch card (signature, product mirror)
A faithful rendering of the macOS approval card inside a black bezel with a notch slot over a dimmed editor plane.
- **Card:** dark glass (card at 94%), 20px radius, 1px white-10% border, card shadow, fixed label grid: mascot face in a draining blue countdown ring, question, agent and folder meta, risk pill, command well in mono.
- **Scope radiogroup:** four segments (Once, 10 min, All folders, Always) in a 12px dark well; the latched segment is a light chip (card-ink face, screen-black legend, small drop); pressing travels 1px; a disabled segment is 35% opacity and struck through.
- **Actions:** graphite Deny and system-blue Allow keys (12px radius, 40px tall, 1fr / 1.3fr) with kbd hints (esc, return).
- **Motion:** the card drops from the slot (translateY -24px, scale 0.92/0.6, clip-path closing to the notch width) over 420ms on the out curve, and retracts the same way; a deny shakes the face 3px over 360ms.

### Browser surfaces
- **Selection:** 35% signal orange over transparent, ink text.
- **Focus ring:** 2px signal outline, 3px offset, 4px radius, global on `:focus-visible`.
- **Scrollbar:** 12px; plate-lo track, ink-3 thumb with a 3px plate-lo border and 6px radius; `scrollbar-color` set to match.
- **Caret and accent-color:** signal orange.

### Motion grammar
- **Mechanical curve** (`cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 0.1, 1)`): key travel at 90ms, scope press, the deny shake.
- **Out curve** (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`): card drop and retract (420ms), FAQ toggle (220ms), face swap (200ms).
- Colour changes are 160ms ease.
- **Reduced motion:** smooth scrolling is turned off; card, face, key, scope and FAQ transitions and animations are removed; the demo does not auto-cycle and the countdown ring stays full.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every gap, inset and texture pitch on the 8px panel grid (`--u`).
- **Do** use exactly one signal-orange key per view, for the primary action, with a charcoal legend.
- **Do** separate regions with engraved rules (1px rule plus a light lip) and cut panels into the plate with `--recess`.
- **Do** give every key press depth: 2px travel and the key-down shadow on the mechanical curve.
- **Do** mark status with lamps (8px, green on, grey off), never with coloured text alone.
- **Do** set legends condensed and heavy (stretch 88-94%, weight 600-660) and reading text at 420 normal width.
- **Do** use `minmax(0, 1fr)` for every collapsing grid track.
- **Do** keep the dark scheme a token swap of plate and ink; geometry and signal stay put.
- **Do** depict the notch card exactly as the macOS product draws it, system-blue Allow key included.

### Don't:
- **Don't** let anything on the aluminium float or glow: no large ambient shadows, no blur halos, no hover lift. Those belong only to the bezel and card.
- **Don't** use orange for secondary keys, borders or decoration beyond the focus ring, caret, selection and the mascot.
- **Don't** carry Allow blue, card glass or editor syntax colours outside the device bezel.
- **Don't** use monospace for labels, headings or decoration.
- **Don't** add travel, drops or auto-cycling when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set.
- **Don't** fill the plate with full-bleed texture; the grille is a bounded field.
