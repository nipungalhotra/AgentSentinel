Privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

loggdin is made and run by an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described on this page. You can reach us as described under Contact.

The short version

The loggdin app keeps everything on your computer and sends us nothing. This website only collects your email address, and only if you join the waitlist.

The app

loggdin runs on your computer. Permission requests from your AI agents travel over 127.0.0.1 and a private Unix socket protected by a per-user token. The app has no account, sends no telemetry, and does not upload your commands, files or decisions to us or anyone else.

Your rules, approvals and audit log are stored on your machine: in ~/Library/Application Support/AgentSentinel on macOS, %APPDATA%\Loggdin on Windows and ~/.config/loggdin on Linux. Delete that folder to erase them.

If you set up a webhook, loggdin sends the request details you chose to the address you configured. That data goes to your destination, not to us, and nothing is sent unless you set one up.

This website

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

The waitlist

What we collect: only the email address you enter.

Why: to tell you when the signed Mac app is ready, and occasionally about major releases or plan changes. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Legal basis: your consent, which you give by submitting the form (GDPR Article 6(1)(a); section 6 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it: the list is stored and sent through a third-party email service. We'll name that service on this page before the form starts collecting addresses. It may store data outside your country, for example in the United States.

How long: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If we close the waitlist without launching, we'll delete the list.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link that removes you straight away. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In India, you can raise a grievance with us first, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

loggdin is a developer tool and isn't directed at anyone under 18. Please don't join the waitlist if you're under 18.

Changes

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If you're on the waitlist and the change matters, we'll also email you.

Contact and grievances

For privacy requests or grievances, email us: a dedicated privacy address is being set up and will be listed here. Please don't post personal details in public GitHub issues. For general questions, use GitHub Issues.

loggdin is an independent project and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Anysphere or GitHub.
