Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The app

loggdin runs on your computer. Permission requests from your AI agents travel over 127.0.0.1 and a private Unix socket protected by a per-user token. The app has no account, sends no telemetry, and does not upload your commands, files or decisions anywhere.

Your rules, approvals and audit log are stored on your machine: in ~/Library/Application Support/AgentSentinel on macOS, %APPDATA%\Loggdin on Windows and ~/.config/loggdin on Linux. Delete that folder to erase them.

If you set up a webhook, loggdin sends the request details you configured to the address you chose. Nothing is sent unless you set one up.

This website

The waitlist form collects only your email address, so we can tell you when the signed Mac app is ready and about major releases. We don't sell it or share it for marketing. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

The site itself uses no cookies and no analytics. It's hosted on a static web host, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.
